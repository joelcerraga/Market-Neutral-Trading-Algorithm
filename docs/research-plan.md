## What the completed research establishes

The baseline and graph strategies lose money at default costs. The graph underperforms in development; its small validation improvement has an interval spanning zero and shrinks under matched-gross control. Keep those results.

H0 mean persistence substantially overlaps mean correlation. H1 descriptors are less redundant under the declared controls but more sensitive to window length. The transferred H0-maximum control model has negative validation R². No primary feature or window has been selected after the comparison, and no topology trading overlay has been introduced.

The four experiment protocols are local hash records, not external preregistration. Historical baseline outcomes were known before the graph protocol, and both strategies’ outcomes were known before the topology protocol. The feature protocol was locked before historical topology extraction.

## Milestone 5: completed final evaluation

The final protocol fixed graph τ = 1, baseline and matched-gross controls, 22 cases, the same costs/execution, three-mean family inference and four necessary evidence conditions before acquiring 2020–2025. Topology remained a descriptor because the previous stage did not establish a defensible overlay rule.

Default-cost CAGR was −5.57% for baseline and −4.80% for graph. The graph-minus-baseline arithmetic mean was +0.73 percentage points, with a family-adjusted interval spanning −2.14 to +3.85. None of the necessary evidence conditions was met. The entire test period is now observed and is unavailable as a fresh evaluation for later model changes.

The independent final data vintage passed the overlap audit. All original source hashes and development-fitted topology models remain unchanged. An empty sensitivity-ledger artifact was repaired with verified atomic writes and a full replay, retaining the initial failure evidence and every unaffected output hash.

As Bailey et al. (2017) explain in *The probability of backtest overfitting*, repeated strategy selection can make a favourable historical result misleading. The final record retains all candidates and the negative outcome, rather than retuning after the test.

Record actual setbacks, design forks, mitigation and unresolved limitations for each milestone in `docs/milestone-decisions.md`. Do not manufacture failures or claim that a negative economic finding has been fixed through better wording.
